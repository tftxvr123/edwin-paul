Methodology

How I Approach Product Problems

A disciplined 9-step framework ensuring every technical investment solves a real customer challenge and produces measurable business impact.

STAGE 01

Understand

Who is the user?

Uncover the primary persona, their environment, motivations, and operational constraints.

STAGE 02

Discover

What problem are they experiencing?

Observe friction, support tickets, and drop-off points rather than just taking requested features at face value.

STAGE 03

Validate

How do we know this is a real problem?

Triangulate qualitative feedback with log telemetry, market size (TAM), and frequency of occurrence.

STAGE 04

Define

What outcome are we trying to achieve?

Establish precise success metrics upfront (e.g., 25% faster setup, 30% fewer support tickets).

STAGE 05

Prioritize

What should we build first?

Separate core functional unlocks from nice-to-haves using customer impact and technical feasibility.

STAGE 06

Build

What is the smallest useful MVP?

Deploy the leanest increment that delivers authentic end-to-end value to validate key assumptions.

STAGE 07

Measure

What metrics define success?

Track activation rates, drop-offs, and error rates via live dashboards immediately after launch.

STAGE 08

Learn

What does user behaviour tell us?

Compare hypothesized outcomes against actual analytics data and direct user responses.

STAGE 09

Iterate

What should we improve next?

Feed insights back into the product backlog to continuously hone experience and business yield.
